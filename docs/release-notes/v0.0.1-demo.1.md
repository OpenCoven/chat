**OpenCoven Chat Demo** — a self-contained preview of the Familiars Redesign v2 chat surface. It connects to nothing and keeps nothing: every reply, decision, and metric is local mock data, and a relaunch resets everything.

### What it shows

- **The ward at the centre of the chat.** Each familiar's Access tab lays out what it may do alone, what it must ask about, and the only paths it may change, with the five-property Familiar Contract underneath.
- **Held actions.** A familiar that reaches its must-ask boundary stops in the thread and waits — approve with `⌘⏎`, decline with `⌘⌫` — and "Needs you" collects everything waiting across familiars.
- **A composer that warns first.** Drafting something inside a familiar's must-ask tier shows *Held for approval* before you send.
- **Summoning.** New familiar… → Researcher / Builder / Manager / Communicator, a suggested name, the ward it inherits, one click.
- **`@` mentions.** Tag another familiar into a conversation; it answers in its own voice under its own mark.
- **A familiar's screen.** The monitor button opens the screen panel; the watch view frames it in the recording colour with a running clock. (The frame the real feed will drop into — no screen data yet.)
- Reasoning cards, the evidence-map lightbox, `⌘K` search, `[` / `]` and hairline edge toggles for the rails, and the earlier Messages-shaped surface at `?demo=messages` for comparison.

### Install (macOS, Apple silicon)

Open the DMG and drag **OpenCoven Chat Demo** to Applications. It installs alongside the real OpenCoven Chat (`ai.opencoven.chat.demo`). The app is signed with a Developer ID certificate but **not notarized**: on first launch macOS may say it can't verify the developer — right-click → Open once, or allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Built from `release/demo-0.0.1-demo.1` with `pnpm app:build:demo`.
